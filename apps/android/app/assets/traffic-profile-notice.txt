Changes: extracted encrypted TLS-record sizes, directions and relative timestamps;
excluded incomplete TCP prefixes and two initial encrypted records per direction;
selected eligible paired traces; removed network addresses and packet contents;
exported bounded metadata sequences. Runtime applies independent padding budgets
and availability overrides. This is a replay baseline, not the authors' original
packet captures, a corporate/POS profile, or evidence of undetectability.
