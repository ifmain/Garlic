Garlic includes modified ipstack1.0.1 and tun2proxy0.8.7.
Their authors, copyright notices and licenses are retained in Ipstack.txt
(Apache-2.0) and Tun2proxy.txt(MIT).
Upstream sources: https://github.com/narrowlink/ipstack
https://github.com/tun2proxy/tun2proxy

Local modifications preserve previously applied UDP parsing/cancellation,
pooled connection generation, TCP buffer and Windows socket fixes, and add
application-visible IPv4 UDP failures plus an absolute first-response deadline
for unanswered QUIC Initial packets. See vendor/README.md in Garlic's source.
